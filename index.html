POWER GUIDE

Train the right gear.

Approximate power zones based on your current FTP. Zwift scales its workouts from the FTP saved in your profile, so keep that number current. The workout's prescribed intervals take priority over these general ranges.

Your recorded maximum heart rate (— bpm) is not enough to set precise heart-rate zones. Heart rate lags during intervals and varies with fatigue, heat, and hydration; use it as context, not a target to chase.

This is a general training template, not medical advice or a guaranteed FTP result. A substantial FTP increase over 12 weeks is ambitious and depends on training history, recovery, health, and accurate testing. Reduce or skip intensity if unwell, unusually fatigued, or in pain; consult a qualified clinician before starting if you have health concerns.
